### Featured Project

#### 🛠️ [ARAR-C3D — Civil 3D 2026 Automation & BIM Add-in](https://github.com/adrianrruiz/ARAR-C3D)
Native **C# (.NET 8 / WPF)** ribbon suite for **Autodesk Civil 3D 2026 (x64)** focused on highway production, corridor QA/QC, and OpenBIM classification:

- **OpenBIM Classification (`RCEclass` & `IFC 4x3 ADD2`):** Interactive dual-tree WPF classifier applying official Spanish Road Network (*Red de Carreteras del Estado*) Property Sets (`RCE_CLA`) and `IFC::IfcExportAs` mappings to 3D Civil 3D elements.
- **Interactive Corridor Section Viewer:** Modeless WPF cross-section & longitudinal profile inspector with live plan cursor tracking, cut/fill earthwork hatching, pavement layer area breakdown, **automated anomaly detection**, 8-point staking table, persistent DWG bookmarks, and clean DWG/PNG export.
- **Road Markings & Civil Production:** Automated 2D/3D road markings, solid-to-TIN surface projection, native chevron hatching (*cebreados*), alignment/volume tables, and `.xlsx` export via `ClosedXML`.
- **Engineering Quality:** Modular architecture (`Core`, `Models`, `Services`, `Tools`, `UI`), automated `xUnit` unit tests, and `.bundle` autoloader installer.
